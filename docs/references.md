# References

The papers behind the layers, geometries and numerical methods of the package.
Cite the paper that introduced a layer when you publish results obtained with
it.

## Networks and layers

(ref-huang2017)=
**[Huang & Van Gool 2017]** Z. Huang, L. Van Gool. A Riemannian network for
SPD matrix learning. *AAAI Conference on Artificial Intelligence* 31, 2017.
[doi:10.1609/aaai.v31i1.10866](https://doi.org/10.1609/aaai.v31i1.10866),
[arXiv:1608.04233](https://arxiv.org/abs/1608.04233).
SPDNet: BiMap, ReEig, LogEig.

(ref-brooks2019)=
**[Brooks et al. 2019]** D. Brooks, O. Schwander, F. Barbaresco,
J.-Y. Schneider, M. Cord. Riemannian batch normalization for SPD neural
networks. *NeurIPS*, 2019.
[arXiv:1909.02414](https://arxiv.org/abs/1909.02414).
Batch normalization with the Karcher mean and an SPD bias.

(ref-kobler2022)=
**[Kobler et al. 2022]** R. Kobler, J. Hirayama, Q. Zhao, M. Kawanabe. SPD
domain-specific batch normalization to crack interpretable unsupervised domain
adaptation in EEG. *NeurIPS*, 2022.
[arXiv:2206.01323](https://arxiv.org/abs/2206.01323).
Normalization by a scalar dispersion.

(ref-wang2025)=
**[Wang et al. 2025]** R. Wang, S. Jin, Z. Chen, X. Luo, X.-J. Wu. Learning to
normalize on the SPD manifold under Bures–Wasserstein geometry. *CVPR*, 2025.
[arXiv:2504.00660](https://arxiv.org/abs/2504.00660),
[code](https://github.com/jjscc/GBWBN).
GBWBN.

(ref-katsman2023)=
**[Katsman et al. 2023]** I. Katsman, E. M. Chen, S. Holalkere, A. Asch,
A. Lou, S.-N. Lim, C. De Sa. Riemannian residual neural networks.
*NeurIPS*, 2023.
[arXiv:2310.10013](https://arxiv.org/abs/2310.10013),
[code](https://github.com/CUAI/Riemannian-Residual-Neural-Networks).
RResNet.

(ref-yue2026)=
**[Yue et al. 2026]** Res2SPDNet. *CVPR Findings*, 2026.
[code](https://github.com/tewubieiDD/Res2SPDNet).
Residual branch with parallel transport, congruence "addition".

(ref-tyler1987)=
**[Tyler 1987]** D. E. Tyler. A distribution-free M-estimator of multivariate
scatter. *The Annals of Statistics* 15(1), 1987.
[doi:10.1214/aos/1176350263](https://doi.org/10.1214/aos/1176350263).
